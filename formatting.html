THE DAY I STOPPED RUSHING

A tiny story about taking the long way home

18 September, after class

The detail I almost missed

A small choice can change the mood
End note: take the long way sometimes

I was about to sprint for the bus when I noticed the sky had turned the exact colour of a peach iced tea. So I took the long way home. That choice felt weirdly brave, even though it was just a twenty-minute walk. I bought a snack, called my friend, and highlighted the part of the day that actually felt like mine.

Real-life details matter: the corner table had H2O on the menu, and my confidence went from 0 to 1003 after one good song. The walk was worth it. I needed to rush home.
